# Generals 2

A Red Alert 3 mod started in 2015, originally meant to recreate Command & Conquer: Generals 2, which EA stopped developing in 2013.

## Where it stands now

Limited by the RA3 engine and the length of the development cycle, the project no longer aims for a 1:1 recreation of Generals 2. It has moved toward experimental content that the author, TRINITY_遗忘, wants to build.

## Assets

Every model and texture in the game was hand-made by TRINITY_遗忘 from 2017 onward, based on image and video references. None of it comes from unpacking official game files.
