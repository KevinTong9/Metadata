# Insurrection

A Red Alert 3 mod that brings the Uprising expansion to the base game: its units, its maps and its balance changes, ready to use in online matches.

## Contents

- All 11 buildable Uprising units: Cryo Legionnaire, Future Tank X-1, Harbinger Gunship, Pacifier FAV, Archer Maiden, Giga-Fortress, Steel Ronin, Desolator, Grinder, Mortar Cycle and Reaper.
- The 28 Uprising maps.
- The balance changes Uprising made to vanilla units.
- Uprising's hidden units that never appeared in regular play: Chief Scientist, Kenji's mech and the Giant Bear.

## History

Bibber built it on Upheaval 1.16 and carried over the data changes Uprising made to vanilla content. In 2015 jkjuuu took over with Bibber's permission, handling balance, while 矛式迷你潜艇 wrote the code from 1.02 up to 1.44; suvzi maintained it after that. The current 2.01 is released by Yary, together with the Uprising UI source (AptUI).

## Fixed in 2.01

- Future Tank X-1's main weapons fire in turn, and it gains experience normally.
- Pacifier FAV can no longer move freely after being teleported by the Chronosphere in Artillery Mode.
- Giga-Fortress uses the longitudinal firing effect in air mode.
- A destroyed Mortar Cycle shows its pilot flying out.
- Grinder can no longer one-shot buildings on the water.
- Desolator's spray now has the water column effect.
- Archer Maiden's Barrage Shot spreads evenly.

## Known issues

- Cryo Legionnaire is missing the freezing beams on both sides when attacking.
- Future Tank X-1's Riot Beam cannot draw an "X" shape.
- Harbinger Gunship cannot circle with a very large radius, and it descends from the sky once construction finishes.
- Reaper Turret's ability cannot use the building cursor.
